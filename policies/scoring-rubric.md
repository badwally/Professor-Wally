## Rule

Evaluate all learner responses consistently using this rubric framework.

### Study Question Scoring (0 or 1)
- **Correct (1.0)**: Answer includes the key facts/terms and demonstrates understanding
- **Incorrect (0.0)**: Answer is missing, wrong, or demonstrates fundamental misunderstanding
- **Partial credit is not awarded** for study questions — they are factual recall

### Mastery Question Scoring (0.0 - 1.0, 5-point scale)

| Score | Label | Criteria |
|-------|-------|----------|
| 1.0 | Excellent | Covers all rubric points, demonstrates original thinking, connects to broader context |
| 0.8 | Strong | Covers most rubric points, clear reasoning, minor gaps |
| 0.6 | Adequate | Covers core rubric points, some reasoning gaps, surface-level connections |
| 0.4 | Developing | Missing key rubric points, reasoning is unclear or incomplete |
| 0.2 | Beginning | Significant gaps, demonstrates fundamental misunderstanding |
| 0.0 | No attempt | No response or completely off-topic |

### Mastery Question Evaluation Dimensions
For each mastery question response, evaluate across:
1. **Accuracy**: Are the facts and concepts correct?
2. **Depth**: Beyond surface-level, explains mechanisms
3. **Connections**: Links to related concepts
4. **Examples**: Relevant, original examples
5. **Clarity**: Logical structure, clear expression

The score is the average of these five dimensions.

### Tutor Session Scoring (5 dimensions, each 0-100)
1. **Recall**: Can they retrieve relevant facts?
2. **Understanding**: Do they explain mechanisms, not just terms?
3. **Application**: Can they use the concept in a new context?
4. **Integration**: Do they connect to other concepts/modules?
5. **Teaching**: Can they explain it clearly to others?

Session quality = average of all five dimensions / 100.

### Feedback Guidelines
- Always provide specific feedback referencing what was strong and what was missing
- For incorrect answers, explain the correct answer and why
- For partial answers, acknowledge what was correct before addressing gaps
- Reference the rubric criteria when explaining scores
- Adapt feedback tone to `learner.preferences.feedback_style`

## Exceptions

- During initial orientation (first session on a module), scoring is relaxed — focus on engagement not accuracy
- Self-rated SRS scores (0-5 quality) are accepted at face value without tutor evaluation
